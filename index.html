About

I'm an Ontology Engineer who transforms unstructured information into interconnected knowledge. My journey took me from Peru to Taiwan for business studies, then to Germany where I discovered my passion for semantic web technologies. After 4+ years leading research projects at Fraunhofer IIS and TH Nuremberg — building ontologies for the hydrogen economy, developing NLP pipelines, and publishing at top venues like ISWC — I've transitioned to industry as a Technical Consultant at metaphacts, helping organizations harness the power of knowledge graphs.
